Key Insights: Multimodal Optical & SAR Radar Feature Fusion
01 / DUAL-STREAM FEATURE FUSION
Complementary Physical Sensing Domains
Optical sensors detect surface solar reflectance and chlorophyll absorption, while SAR microwaves measure surface roughness, moisture, and geometric structure. Concatenating 1536-dim optical with 1536-dim SAR features yields a 3072-dim vector that resolves spectral ambiguities.
02 / ALL-WEATHER RELIABILITY
Overcoming Cloud Cover with SAR Radar
Optical satellite monitoring frequently suffers from cloud obscuration (up to 60% of European land coverage). The SAR branch penetrates atmospheric haze, fog, and clouds, ensuring continuous, operational land observation in all weather conditions.
03 / EFFICIENCY VS RESNET-50
Higher Accuracy with Balanced Complexity
Even with dual-stream multimodal fusion, the combined EfficientNet-B3 network uses 24.0M parameters—less than the single 24.6M ResNet-50—while outperforming it by +1.80% in top accuracy and delivering explainable Grad-CAM across both physical sensors.
