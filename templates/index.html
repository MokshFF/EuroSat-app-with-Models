Key Findings

  • Fusion model outperforms both single-modality models by combining spectral + structural cues
  • Optical excels on vegetated classes — rich spectral signatures
  • SAR bands better distinguish structural classes (Industrial, Residential, Highway)
  • AnnualCrop ↔ PermanentCrop and Herbaceous ↔ Pasture are the hardest pairs
  • ImageNet transfer learning provides strong initialization for satellite imagery

Dataset & Setup

  • EuroSAT: Sentinel-2 multispectral patches (64×64 px), 10 classes
  • Optical: Bands B4, B3, B2 (True color RGB)
  • SAR-simulated: Bands B8 (NIR), B11, B12 (SWIR)
  • Balanced: 100 patches × 10 classes = 1,000 total
  • Split: 60% train · 20% val · 20% test (stratified)
  • Batch size: 32 · Max 60 epochs per model
